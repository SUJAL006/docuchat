Company Handbook: Remote Work Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period
are eligible to apply for remote work arrangements. Approval is granted by
the employee's direct manager in consultation with HR.

2. Work Hours
Remote employees are expected to be available during core hours of
10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in their local time zone. Outside of core hours,
employees may set a flexible schedule as long as they complete 8 hours
of work per day.

3. Equipment
The company provides a laptop, monitor, and a one-time stipend of $500
for home office setup. Employees are responsible for maintaining a
stable internet connection of at least 50 Mbps.

4. Communication
Remote employees must be reachable via Slack during working hours and
should update their status when stepping away for more than 30 minutes.
Weekly one-on-one check-ins with managers are mandatory.

5. Performance Reviews
Remote employees are evaluated using the same performance criteria as
in-office employees. There is no penalty for remote status in promotion
or compensation decisions.

6. Security
Employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems and
may not work from public, unsecured Wi-Fi networks without VPN
protection. Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled.

7. Travel Requirement
Remote employees are required to travel to the main office for
quarterly team meetings, at the company's expense. Advance notice of
at least 3 weeks will be given for these meetings.
